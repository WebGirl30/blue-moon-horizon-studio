CONTACT FORM
The form is front-end only right now. It displays a confirmation message but does not email submissions. It can later be connected to Formspree, Netlify Forms, EmailJS, or your hosting provider's form service.

WHAT TO CUSTOMIZE
- Replace the placeholder contact email/response-time text on contact.html.
- Replace the # placeholders in footer social links with your actual social URLs.
- Adjust prices in pricing.html if needed.
- Replace portfolio mockups with screenshots of your real work when ready.
